The 4-Step Universal Routine

01

Dry Debris Wipe

Always knock off loose dirt, dried mud, and street dust with a soft horsehair brush before introducing any moisture or foam.

02

Targeted Cleansing

Match cleaners strictly to material chemistry. Never machine-wash leather or submerge heat-bonded midsoles in boiling water.

03

Nourish & Condition

Full-grain leather and traditional mojaris require natural oil or cream emulsions to prevent fibrous drying, stiffening, and cracking.

04

Shape & Air Storage

Insert aromatic cedar shoe trees to wick sweat moisture, neutralize odor bacteria, and maintain the factory toe-box contour.

Material-Specific Care Protocols

Full-Grain & Calfskin

OXFORDS, DERBIES, LOAFERS, CHELSEA
  • •Wipe down with a damp microfiber cloth after every 2 wears.
  • •Apply lanolin-based leather balm every 6 to 8 weeks.
  • •Buff vigorously with horsehair brush to awaken natural oils.
  • ×Avoid: Direct heat radiators or hair dryers (dries fiber glue).

Suede & Nubuck Leathers

CHUKKA BOOTS, SUEDE LOAFERS, DESERT
  • •Never wipe with wet cloths; water leaves permanent rings.
  • •Use a crepe rubber brush in one direction to raise nap.
  • •Rub scuff stains with a dedicated rubber eraser block.
  • ✓Pro Tip: Mist with fluoropolymer waterproof spray every 60 days.

Canvas & Engineered Mesh

RUNNING SHOES, LIFESTYLE, VANS, SNEAKERS
  • •Dilute gentle eco-detergent in lukewarm water with soft bristles.
  • •Clean white rubber sidewalls with a melamine eraser sponge.
  • •Remove laces and soak separately in soapy disinfectant.
  • ×Avoid: Tumble dryers; intense heat warps the EVA foam geometry.

High Heels & Stilettos

STILETTOS, BLOCK HEELS, PLATFORMS, PUMPS
  • •Swap heel tips as soon as the inner steel pin becomes exposed.
  • •Affix adhesive rubber traction pads to slick outsole leather.
  • •Store each shoe in its own velvet sleeve to protect the thin stems.
  • ✓Pro Tip: Stuff pointed toe-boxes with acid-free tissue.

Ethnic Vegetable Leather

PUNJABI JUTTIS, MOJARIS, KOLHAPURI CHAPPALS
  • •Rub mustard or castor oil on inner collar seam before first wear.
  • •Maintain Kolhapuri leather flexibility with quarterly light oil coats.
  • •Store metallic zardozi embroidery wrapped in pure cotton muslin.
  • ×Avoid: Heavy monsoon puddle exposure (raw leather harbors mildew).

Cork, Jute & EVA Foam

BIRKENSTOCKS, ESPADRILLES, SLIDES, CLOGS
  • •Coat exposed cork rims with cork sealant whenever finish fades.
  • •Never soak jute espadrille braids in water; clean with dry soap froth.
  • •Wash molded EVA recovery slides with cold water and gentle soap.
  • ×Avoid: Leaving foam shoes in hot locked cars (heat causes shrinkage).

Footwear Triage: Rapid Field Fixes

How do I remove water & salt lines from leather?

Mix equal parts white vinegar and distilled water. Dab gently on salt lines with a cotton cloth, allow to dry completely at room temperature, then replenish with leather balm.

How do I eliminate stubborn odor bacteria?

Insert activated bamboo charcoal sachets or raw red cedar shoe trees. For rapid deodorization, insert unbrewed dry green tea bags or baking soda in the toe box overnight.

How can I reduce sneaker toe-box creases?

Pack the shoe tightly with rolled socks. Place a damp washcloth across the toe box and glide a hot clothing iron in gentle circles for 10-15 seconds. Let cool with trees firmly locked in.

How to stop new footwear from heel-rubbing?

Glide beeswax, clear stick deodorant, or candle wax across the inner heel collar counter to eliminate friction until the leather naturally molds to your calcaneus bone.
