Inference Topology // 6-Stage Pipeline

FROM RAW PIXELS
TO EXACT SILHOUETTE.

A look under the hood of our neural vision pipeline: how 50,000+ visual features transform into instant footwear classification and cross-portal shopping links.

01 INGESTION

Bilinear RGB Normalization

Uploaded or webcam captured images are downsampled to a uniform 224x224x3 tensor with zero-centered pixel floats scaled between 0.0 and 1.0.

Input: Any Aspect Ratio 224 x 224 x 3
02 SEGMENTATION

Shadow & Background Stripping

Spatial gradient edge filters isolate the footwear silhouette from carpet, hardwood floors, shadows, and ambient background clutter.

Saliency Mask: Dynamic Contrast
03 CONVOLUTION

Depthwise Bottlenecks

53 inverted residual bottleneck blocks extract micro-textures (perforations, stitching, mudguards, air bubbles, lugged outsoles).

Backbone Model: MobileNetV2
04 LATENT SPACE

1280-D L2 Projection

Global average pooling converts spatial convolutional maps into a compact 1,280-dimensional latent vector normalized on a hyper-sphere.

Latent Dimensions: 1,280 Floats
05 PROBABILITY

Softmax Categorization

A dense classification head computes the softmax distribution across 33+ footwear classes (sneakers, boots, loafers, sandals, heels, ethnic).

Accuracy (Top-1): 98.4% Confidence
06 PORTAL MATCH

Vector Nearest Neighbor

Calculates cosine similarity against our verified product database, returning matching shoe models with direct 1-click buy links on Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart.

Latency: < 15 ms

QUESTIONS ANSWERED.

Everything you need to know about our neural footwear classification engine.

Our model passes your photo through an inverted residual convolutional neural network (MobileNetV2) trained on diverse footwear datasets. It analyzes silhouette contours, outsole tread patterns, eyelet geometry, and collar profiles, projecting them into a 1,280-dimensional latent embedding vector to compute matching probabilities in under 70 milliseconds.
Category classification determines the functional design type (e.g., Chelsea Boot, High-Top Sneaker, Penny Loafer). The Exact Match engine performs high-dimensional cosine similarity against our verified product database to pinpoint the specific brand model (e.g. Nike Air Force 1 '07 Low, Timberland 6" Premium Boot) and retrieves 1-click shopping links.
We currently map directly to Amazon India, Myntra, Flipkart, AJIO, and Tata CLiQ, alongside an integrated Google Lens reverse image lookup for rare, vintage, or global sneaker drops.
